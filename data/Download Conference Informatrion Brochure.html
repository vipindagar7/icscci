Call for Papers

ICSCCI 2027 invites authors to submit original, unpublished research through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (Microsoft CMT), promoting innovative thinking and practical applications that contribute to intelligent, sustainable, and future-ready digital infrastructures for smart societies.

Scope

ICSCCI 2027 focuses on the convergence of next-generation connectivity technologies, including 5G/6G networks, the Internet of Things (IoT), edge and cloud computing, and intelligent communication systems integrated with advanced computational intelligence and artificial intelligence techniques such as machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and Agentic AI.The conference promotes interdisciplinary research in smart communication networks, edge intelligence, cybersecurity, trustworthy AI, intelligent automation, and digital transformation, addressing key challenges related to scalability, security, resource optimization, efficiency, and adaptability.

ICSCCI 2027 welcomes original theoretical, experimental, and application-oriented contributions from academia, industry, and research organizations. The application domains include smart cities, healthcare, intelligent transportation systems, Industry 4.0/5.0, cyber-physical systems, and sustainable digital infrastructures.
